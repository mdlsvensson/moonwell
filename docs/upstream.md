# Upstream workarounds

The released Moonwell 0.2.5 builds with `Moonwell.CSharpLua` 2.0.4-moonwell.9, `Moonwell.CSharpLua.CoreSystem`
2.0.3-moonwell.2 and `Moonwell.War3Net.Build` 6.1.2-moonwell.3; main builds with 2.0.4-moonwell.12, 2.0.3-moonwell.4 and
6.1.2-moonwell.4, so the fork's fixes from 2.0.4-moonwell.10 and 2.0.3-moonwell.3 on (part of C25, C28 to C32, C34 to
C40) are on main only.

What Moonwell does because War3Net or CSharp.lua (War3Net.CSharpLua 2.0.4) behaves in a way it has to work around.
Moonwell builds with its own fork of CSharp.lua, `mdlsvensson/CSharp.lua` (decision 33), through its packages on nuget.org,
`Moonwell.CSharpLua` and `Moonwell.CSharpLua.CoreSystem` (published by the fork's `publish.yml`); a row marked "fixed in the
fork" is no longer a problem there. Nothing in the fork has been sent
to Drake53 or yanghuan yet.
It also builds with a fork of War3Net, `mdlsvensson/War3Net`, through the packages `Moonwell.War3Net.IO.Slk`,
`Moonwell.War3Net.Build.Core` and `Moonwell.War3Net.Build` (only those three differ from War3Net's); a War3Net row marked
"fixed in the fork" is no longer a problem there, and nothing in that fork has been sent to Drake53 either.
Each entry says what upstream does, where Moonwell handles it, the upstream issue if there is one, and what removing
the workaround would take. The evidence for each is in the workspace's `docs/game-facts.md`.

When a workaround is added or removed, update this list in the same commit.
